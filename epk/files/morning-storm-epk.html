The Band

Morning Storm is a stoner metal three-piece from Blumenau, southern Brazil, forged on stage since 2015. The sound runs heavy and urgent — stoner groove charged with crossover thrash and hardcore punk, raised on the Brazilian lineage of Sepultura and Ratos de Porão as much as on Pantera, Down and Suicidal Tendencies. The lyrics cut between social critique and life as the band actually lives it.

After the 2021 EP Dark Side and a steady run of singles, 2026 is the band's biggest year yet: a new EP is in the works, with lead single and video “Mars & Venus” out in July. In October, Morning Storm opens for RODOX — the new band of Rodolfo Abrantes (ex-Raimundos). In January 2027, the band crosses the Atlantic for its first European tour.
